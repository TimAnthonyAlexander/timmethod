# W8-06 · The honesty page

**Wave:** 8 — Tim Method Engine
**Status:** open
**Depends on:** W8-04
**Spec:** §17.1

## Goal
An in-app page stating what is measured, how well, and what is deliberately not claimed.

## Why
The whole thesis is measurement discipline. An app that refuses to show absolute joint angles should explain why. Guideline 1.4.1 also wants methodology disclosed for accuracy claims.

## Do
- [ ] What velocity loss estimates and what it does not, with the CV numbers stated plainly (15–22% bench, 26–34% squat)
- [ ] Why there is no 1RM estimate: the minimum-velocity-threshold method systematically overestimates back squat 1RM, with an explicit "exercise caution" conclusion in the meta-analysis
- [ ] Why ROM is a percentage of your own range and not degrees: ≥21.5° elbow flexion error across every model tested
- [ ] Why no tempo coaching: g = 0.05 for eccentric duration on hypertrophy, and the 2026 ACSM Position Stand declines to recommend a tempo at all
- [ ] Why no lengthened-partials coaching: the load-bearing number is a non-significant subgroup (SMD −0.28), and the best-powered trial, n = 297, found an interaction of 0.000
- [ ] Which claims are **contested**: frequency for strength (§11.3), rest intervals for hypertrophy (§11.4)
- [ ] Which are **judgment, not evidence**: the ROM ratchet, the deload heuristic, set segmentation
- [ ] Realistic rates of gain, with the caveat that the ubiquitous %-bodyweight table is an expert heuristic, commonly misattributed, and that measured variability runs from −11% to +30% across 287 people on an identical protocol

## Done when
- [ ] Every claim on the page traces to a §20 source or is explicitly marked as judgment
- [ ] It is reachable from Settings in two taps
- [ ] It reads as plain prose, not a disclaimer

## Notes
Write it as something worth reading, not legal cover. It is also the best possible answer to "why is this different from every other rep counter."
