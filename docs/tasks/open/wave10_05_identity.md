# W10-05 · App identity

**Wave:** 10 — Ship Readiness
**Status:** open
**Depends on:** W6-04
**Spec:** §14.1

## Goal
Icon, launch experience and naming that match the rest of the design.

## Why
The fundamentals do not show in a screenshot and all show on a visit.

## Do
- [ ] App icon in the three-token palette, legible at the smallest size, no gradient, no 3D
- [ ] Launch screen that is the app's ground colour, not a splash
- [ ] Settings, About, and the honesty page reachable and coherent
- [ ] Consistent naming: "Tim Method" everywhere, no abbreviations in UI copy
- [ ] Verify at 375pt even though the dev device is a Pro Max

## Done when
- [ ] Icon is legible at 40pt and 1024pt
- [ ] No placeholder assets remain anywhere in the bundle
- [ ] Layout verified at the smallest supported width

## Notes
One idea, executed properly. A bar-path trace is the obvious icon motif and is genuinely specific to this app rather than generic fitness iconography.
